AC Leak Water Damage in South Gate: What to Know

All day long an AC system wrings humidity from indoor air, and the resulting water needs a way out. A pan under the coil catches the drips, and from there a thin pipe empties the pan. Once dust and algae block the drain, water backs up until it runs over the edge of the pan. Attic and closet air handlers send the spill directly into the surrounding ceiling, walls or flooring.

A slow drip causes more harm than most homeowners would guess. Drywall draws the water in, the platform beneath the unit gets saturated, and moisture slides under the floor, with mold often underway before any stain appears. Our team measures how far it traveled, dries the building materials and fixes the damage. You will still need an HVAC technician to clear the drain or fix the unit, and we can work around their visit.

Do This First

Before We Arrive

  1. Shut the System DownSwitch the system off at the thermostat so it stops making condensate. If water is near the air handler's wiring, turn off its breaker as well.
  2. Avoid Wet Wiring and LightsCeiling lights, outlets and the unit itself are off limits while wet, and stay that way until their circuit has been turned off.
  3. Empty the Drain PanIf you can reach the pan safely, soak up or wet-vac the standing water so it stops spilling over. Do not crawl across a wet attic to do it.
  4. Soak Up What You CanMove clothes, boxes and furniture back from the stained area or the closet, catch ceiling drips in a bucket, wipe up hard floors and blot the carpet.
  5. Call Both TradesAn HVAC technician should clear the drain line, and our team should find out where the water went. Leave the system off until it is fixed.
Coverage

Insurance and AC Leak Water Damage

Coverage for an AC leak usually comes down to timing. A sudden pan overflow that you dealt with promptly is the kind of loss homeowners policies often cover. A drip the adjuster believes lasted weeks or months can be classed as gradual leakage or poor maintenance, and the claim denied. The air conditioner repair is typically excluded in both cases. Check your policy, and we can supply readings and photos for the claim.

Common Causes

How It Usually Starts

  • Ice on the evaporator coil melting all at once and overwhelming the pan.
  • A condensate drain line clogged with algae, dust and slime from the coil.
  • Drain pans that sit unevenly or have corroded and split.
  • Units that rely on a condensate pump, when that pump quits or gets unplugged.
  • A dirty filter or low refrigerant, both of which make the coil ice up.
What Is at Risk

What Happens if You Wait

Mold Near the Return

Mold may appear in 24 to 48 hours on damp materials by the air handler, where the return air stream can carry its spores through the ductwork to other rooms.

Nearby Flooring

From the closet, water slips under the door and works below hardwood, laminate or carpet pad. Hardwood cups when it is not dried, while laminate swells for good.

Drywall Below the Air Handler

An attic air handler leaks onto the drywall beneath it. The ceiling stains first, then sags, and a section soaked for days can fall into the hallway or bedroom below.

Attic Insulation

Insulation around an attic unit soaks up overflow and holds it against the ceiling. Once saturated it stops insulating, and replacement is common.

Our Process

Our AC Leak Water Damage Process

1

Find Every Wet Area

Stains show only part of it, so we scan the area by the air handler, the walls or ceiling beside it and the nearby flooring with a thermal camera and moisture meters.

2

Remove Water and Wet Drywall

We extract the water sitting in the carpet, the platform and the pan. Drywall or insulation that is saturated or moldy is cut out so the cavity behind it can air out.

3

Set Drying Equipment

We monitor readings throughout the several days, usually 3 to 5, that the fans and dehumidifiers need. Access to the air handler is kept open for your HVAC technician.

4

Sanitize and Rebuild

Affected surfaces get an antimicrobial treatment, because condensate that has sat for days is rarely clean. Then we replace insulation, drywall, baseboard and flooring and repaint so the area matches.
